To set the board orientation, you need to think about the transformations required to take the board from flat and level and pointing away from you using the right-hand rule to its mounted orientation.
  • First, calculate the angle of rotation about the pitch axis (pitch back is positive)
  • Second, calculate the angle of rotation about the roll axis (roll right is positive)
  • Lastly, calculate the angle of rotation about the yaw axis (yaw left is positive)

4. Mechanical home reference

5. Automatic minimum-motion pulse calibration

After confirming home, secure the representative load, clear travel in both directions, and have the power disconnect within reach before powering the servo. Calibration steps outward in 5 us increments within the configured pulse limits and saves the first reliably moving pulse in each direction after returning to neutral. It does not choose a maximum speed.

Pulse calibration: not run

Stop / Neutral cancels setup motion and requests neutral. If the connection is lost or motion continues, disconnect servo power.

Calibration limits

Each pulse lasts at most 3 seconds and aborts at 15 degrees of raw stage travel or the +/-90 degree setup boundary. Neutral must qualify within 3 seconds and requires at least 2 seconds of heading samples spanning at most 6 degrees with at most 1.5 degrees of averaged drift. A start pulse requires three consecutive advancing 500 ms heading windows and more than 6 degrees of net averaged displacement. Opposite displacement beyond that band aborts. The whole run is limited to 10 minutes.
